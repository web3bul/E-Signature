FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can't find your answer? The signer has a short guided tour the first time you open it.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no account, no trial and no limit on how many documents you sign. Saved files don't carry a watermark.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is opened and edited by your browser on your own device. The signed copy is created there too and saved straight to your downloads.

Is the signature legally valid?

Quill adds an electronic signature, the same kind you'd make with most free signing apps. Many countries accept electronic signatures for everyday agreements, but some documents need a certificate-based digital signature or a handwritten one. If you're unsure, check with the person or organisation receiving the document. This isn't legal advice.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Draw your signature with your finger, tap the page to place it, and save. It works in any modern mobile browser.

Why won't my PDF open?

Password-protected PDFs can't be opened yet. Remove the password with the app that created the file, then try again. Damaged files may also fail to load.

Where are my saved signatures kept?

In this browser only. They aren't sent anywhere, and you can delete one any time with the small ✕ on its card. Clearing your browser data removes them too.
